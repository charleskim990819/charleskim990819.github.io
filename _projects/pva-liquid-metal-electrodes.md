In a collaborative study led by Keun-Young Yook, our team used poly(vinyl alcohol) to modify liquid-metal particles for printing conductive traces. I worked with the team that tested electrodes made with this ink for ECG and EMG recordings.

[Read the paper in Advanced Science](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.78062) · [Open full text and author contributions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13643113/)
