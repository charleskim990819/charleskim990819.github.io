## Concept

A **PVA-carrier water-transfer process** that hydroprints PDA-LM circuits onto arbitrary surfaces — including human skin — in seconds, **without sintering and without primer**. The carrier dissolves on contact with water, leaving a conformal LM trace that is immediately conductive.

## Why it matters

Existing tattoo electronics typically require either (i) skin-side adhesion layers, (ii) post-transfer thermal/mechanical activation, or (iii) organic solvents that limit on-skin deposition. The PDA-LM ink solves all three because its catechol chemistry already provides both adhesion and percolation.

## Phase 1 demonstration

A 4-LED circuit water-transferred onto the back of the hand, driven by an external coin-cell, validates the conductivity-after-transfer claim on a real biological substrate.

## Roadmap

- **Phase 2:** integrate AD5941 EC front-end + BLE for self-contained skin-mounted biosensing
- **Phase 3:** miniaturize to a 3×3 cm tattoo sticker with on-board NFC harvesting
