I print carbon and silver/silver chloride electrodes on a stretchable SEBS substrate to make three-electrode electrochemical cells. I compare devices made with the same pattern and track both the fraction that work and the spread in their response. Those measurements tell me how much I can trust a reading from the next electrode I print.

The companion [portable potentiostat](/projects/5_project/) applies a voltage and reads the resulting current.
