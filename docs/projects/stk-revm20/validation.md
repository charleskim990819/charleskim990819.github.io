# STK RevM20 validation matrix

As of 2026-10-15; hardware RevM20, firmware FW18.

V01-V02 review existing digital design audits. V22 awaits manufacturer review. The other 22 physical test groups have not been run. An unset numerical acceptance criterion blocks performance qualification even after a measurement exists.

CAD_CONFIRMED = confirmed in released CAD/audit records; FW_CONFIRMED = source-reviewed behavior; CALCULATED = stated analytical model; DATASHEET = manufacturer specification under its conditions; BENCH_PENDING = physical evidence required; MANUFACTURER_PENDING = factory disposition required; UNSPECIFIED = numerical requirement not yet set. These labels do not imply factory approval or measured product performance.

## Measurement record

For every run retain board serial, PCB/FW hashes, channel, selector state, range/PGA/OSR/filter/output rate, calibration fingerprint, supply, actual sample temperature/RH, cell/fixture model, instrument identity/calibration/uncertainty, raw data, processing script version, acceptance criterion and outcome. Use independent validation points after fitting.

## Test sequence

1. Factory review and physical assembly/link inspection.
2. USB-only rails/reset/programming, battery and sensor disconnected.
3. Dummy-cell acquisition, DC voltage/current and loop stability.
4. Noise/leakage and electrical cross responses.
5. pH and single-analyte chemical controls, then mixed samples.
6. EIS, environment, mechanical and board-to-board characterization as applicable.
