# STK RevM20 preliminary error budget

As of 2026-10-15; hardware RevM20, firmware FW18.

This is a component-level pre-calibration budget and a plan for residual characterization. There is no justified total product accuracy yet. Keep bounded bias terms, typical noise densities, correlated reference terms and unknown residuals separate. Do not RSS maximum limits as if they were independent standard deviations.

CAD_CONFIRMED = confirmed in released CAD/audit records; FW_CONFIRMED = source-reviewed behavior; CALCULATED = stated analytical model; DATASHEET = manufacturer specification under its conditions; BENCH_PENDING = physical evidence required; MANUFACTURER_PENDING = factory disposition required; UNSPECIFIED = numerical requirement not yet set. These labels do not imply factory approval or measured product performance.

## Requirement feasibility example

A hypothetical 1 nA signal with 1% error permits 10 pA total error. This is an illustration, not an adopted requirement. The ADG704 specified 100 pA terminal leakage limit alone prevents inferring that target from part selection. Actual bias-dependent leakage, temperature drift and post-calibration residual must be characterized.

## Budget closure

Define DeltaI_allow(I,T,B), DeltaE_allow, pH tolerance and EIS magnitude/phase tolerances. Assign budgets to the relevant mechanisms and add instrument/fixture uncertainty. Preserve uncalibrated and independently validated post-calibration results; unresolved contributors remain null rather than zero.
