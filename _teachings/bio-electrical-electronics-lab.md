## Role

Teaching Assistant for **Bio-electrical Electronics Laboratory** at Yonsei University's School of Electrical & Electronic Engineering, from **September 2024 to December 2025**.

## Responsibilities

- Lead weekly **hands-on lab sessions** for undergraduate students
- Mentor student teams through experiments on **biopotential acquisition** (ECG / EMG / EEG front-ends), **electrochemical sensing** (cyclic voltammetry, amperometry), and **embedded data acquisition** (microcontroller-based instrumentation)
- Prepare lab handouts, debug student hardware/firmware issues, and grade lab reports
- Translate research-grade instrumentation knowledge (AD5941 potentiostats, ADS131M08 biopotential ADCs) into pedagogical exercises appropriate for the undergraduate level

## What students learn

- Reading datasheets and translating block diagrams into working circuits
- Acquiring noisy biological signals and validating SNR
- Designing controlled electrochemical experiments (3-electrode cells, reference selection)
- Writing reproducible measurement protocols

This is the course that bridges the abstract circuit-theory background of EE undergrads with the practical realities of building biosensors — the kind of laboratory experience that originally motivated my own move into bioelectronics.
