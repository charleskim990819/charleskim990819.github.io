# Chansoo (Charles) Kim

I am an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University. In the BLISS Lab, I work on liquid-metal inks for stretchable electronics and build instruments for reading signals from printed electrodes.

[Academic website](https://www.charleskim99.com/) · [Project portfolio](https://www.charleskim99.com/projects/) · [CV](https://www.charleskim99.com/cv/)

## Materials and electrodes

| Project                                                                        | What I worked on                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Liquid-metal ink](https://www.charleskim99.com/projects/1_project/)           | Developed a water-based ink that conducts as it dries.                                                      |
| [Water-transfer electronics](https://www.charleskim99.com/projects/2_project/) | Transferred a printed circuit onto the back of a hand and lit four LEDs.                                    |
| [Screen-printed electrodes](_projects/printed-electrodes.md)                   | Compared yield and variation across three-electrode cells printed on stretchable substrates.                |
| [PDRN bio-coating](https://www.charleskim99.com/projects/4_project/)           | Conducted EMG recordings and chemical analysis for a collaborative study of coated tissue interfaces.       |
| [ECG and EMG with liquid-metal electrodes](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.78062) | Helped test electrodes made with a poly(vinyl alcohol)-modified ink for recording heart and muscle signals. |
