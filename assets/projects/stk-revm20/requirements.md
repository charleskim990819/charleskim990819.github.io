# STK RevM20 requirements and design baseline

As of 2026-10-15; hardware RevM20, firmware FW18.

This is a baseline reconstructed from the current implementation, not a claim that all original product requirements were fixed before design. Accuracy, environmental limits and lifetime remain open requirements.

CAD_CONFIRMED = confirmed in released CAD/audit records; FW_CONFIRMED = source-reviewed behavior; CALCULATED = stated analytical model; DATASHEET = manufacturer specification under its conditions; BENCH_PENDING = physical evidence required; MANUFACTURER_PENDING = factory disposition required; UNSPECIFIED = numerical requirement not yet set. These labels do not imply factory approval or measured product performance.
